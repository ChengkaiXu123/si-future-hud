**Top 10 most user-visible problems**
1. **Alert glow pulse missing (#28).** **Fixed 2026-10-21.** When an event arrives, Open flashes the glow in the alert's colour over the sender's segment for 1.8 s, with a double pulse for warnings. Next never flashes the glow.
2. **Paused or blocked detection keeps colour and motion (#5).** **Fixed 2026-10-21.** In Open, paused detection or lost access turns the glow grey (#9a9aa0) and still at 0.35. Next keeps the quota colours breathing. Next's no-reading grey is also #6e6e73, not #9a9aa0.
3. **Dock glow mirrored on two edges (#36).** **Fixed 2026-10-21.** On bottom and left Dynamic docks, Next rotates the canvas, so the glow's agent colours run opposite to the marks.
4. **Light notch-row wings (#56).** **Fixed 2026-10-21.** At a hardware notch with Light appearance, Next draws a compact reminder's wings light, or as light glass. Open keeps the notch row dark (`inNotchRow`).
5. **Quota and reset-credit cards rewritten (#67, #76–#79).** **Fixed 2026-10-21** except the card width and insets (#67). Next changed the copy, metrics, layout and accents:
   - It shows remaining % where Open shows "—"/forecast.
   - Resets more than 24 h away show "52h10m", not weekday and time.
   - Credit expiry is relative, not a date.
   - Accents are #3ddc84/#f59e42, not #6cd8ac/#e9a16d.
   - The main button is tinted, not filled.
   - Cards are 470 wide with 32/18 insets; Open uses 400 with 24 insets.
6. **Full card in the usage panel (#80).** **Fixed 2026-10-21.** While the usage panel is open, a quota or credit event embeds the full detail card at the top of the panel. Open shows a quiet one-line row.
7. **Light and see-through tokens differ across alert and permission cards (#41, #42, #48–#52, #82):** **Fixed 2026-10-21** (light tokens, then the see-through greys and shades and a floor under glass, #42, #48, #57).
   - Allow is #168344 with white ink; Open uses #3ddc84 with black ink.
   - Deny is #ff8a84 / #c4302b.
   - The light-HUD level shades are wrong.
   - The see-through shades and `Theme.under` greys were missing; glass could thin to nothing over a sharp desktop.
   - The +0.2 caption lift on light is missing.
8. **Question card data and input (#92–#94).** **Fixed 2026-10-21.**
   - Multi-select answers reach the client in click order, not offered order.
   - Enter during IME composition submits.
   - Skip, or picking an option, erases typed words.
9. **Plan card (#99, #101).** **Fixed 2026-10-21.** The plan shows raw Markdown (`#`, `**`). Plan feedback being typed is lost when the card unmounts.
10. **Attention animations missing (#75, #83).**
    - The permission hand no longer pulses.
    - The quota flame does not blink.
    - The reset arrow does not spin into a check.
    - The credit arrow does not turn.
    - The look-change cross-fade is also missing (#33).

## Architecture notes (Next)

1. **Business logic in TS UI that Open keeps in Core:**
   - `hud/model.ts:123-185` (`vendorsFromSnapshot`) decides each agent's level (the tightest measured quota) and its working/waiting counts. These drive glow colours and ordering.
   - The quota alert rules are duplicated in three TSX files:
     - the exhausted threshold `usedPercent >= 99.5`: `alerts/CompactAlertView.tsx:14`, `QuotaAlertViews.tsx:220`, `ReminderMark.tsx:72-73`;
     - the status copy: `compactQuotaStatus`;
     - the forecast and reset formatting: `compactDuration`, and `formatResetCountdown`, which uses `Date.now()` instead of the snapshot time as Open's `Countdown.resetLabelCompact(…, now: snapshot.updatedAt)` does.
   - The tool→badge/icon mapping (`PendingEventRows.tsx:25-74`) re-derives what Core's `PermissionRequest.badge`/`symbol` provide.
   - The allow label is hard-coded.

   These belong in Core (or the snapshot), so that HUD, phone and MCP agree.
2. **No shared HUD token set.** At least 10 components declare their own `light ? … : …` literal pairs: ApprovalContent, PendingEventRows, CompactAlertView, CompletionAlertView, QuotaAlertViews, PermissionToolCard, PermissionQuestionCard, PermissionPlanCard, useHUDLayout and panel/theme. Open has one `Theme` and one `PermissionColor`. The drift in #41–#52 and #82 comes from this. A single HUD token module (dark/light × opaque/see-through) mirroring `Theme`/`Theme.under` would fix most colour rows at once.
3. **React Rules of Hooks violation in `permissions/PermissionQuestionCard.tsx`.** `useEffect` at line 239 runs after the early returns at 120-229 (`!canAnswerQuestion`) and 233 (`!q`). If a request keeps its `key` but flips `answersQuestion` or loses its questions, React throws "Rendered fewer/more hooks". The module-level `drafts` map (line 16) is never pruned for withdrawn requests.
4. **Performance:**
   - `HUDGlow.tsx:329-472` (`StaticRim`) recomputes a full w×h pixel loop in JS on the main thread. It reruns on every geometry tick of the 0.4 s island tween, because NotchGlow sets state per motion-value change (`NotchSurface.tsx:189-200`). This happens whenever the static path is active (Reduce Motion, no readings, GPU initialising), e.g. about 0.25 M pixels per frame around an open panel.
   - Soft breathing, which Open runs as a free Core Animation opacity pulse, ran as a WebGPU shader at 8–24 fps on a 60 Hz frame loop. **Fixed 2026-10-21:** it is an opacity animation over a rim drawn once (#20), and the moving effects wake only at their frame rate (#25).
   - There is no shared frame budget across displays (#25).
5. **OS-specific code in shared UI:** none left. `HUDGlassBackground.tsx` sniffed the WebKit user agent to choose the glass renderer; it went with the glass on 2026-10-21 (#57).
6. **Concurrency:** none in this chunk. No provider, account or log reads are made here, so the serial-collection rule is not exercised.
