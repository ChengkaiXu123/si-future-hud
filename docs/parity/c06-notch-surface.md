### Top 10 most user-visible problems

1. **An agent's edit hold cannot be ended by the person** (#37–40). (Escape, pointer, Settings/Statistics and drag end it since 2026-10-21.) In Open, the hold ends when the person:
   - moves the pointer onto the HUD and away again;
   - clicks anywhere off the panel;
   - presses Escape;
   - clicks Settings, Statistics or a session;
   - opens another card;
   - starts a drag.

   In Next only Save, Discard, the extra English "Finish editing" button, a Settings change, or the 2-minute expiry ends it. `open = hover.open || holdActive`, so collapsing does nothing, and the panel stays above the Settings or Statistics window it just opened.
2. **The panel's account sections differ** (#85–87). **Mostly fixed 2026-10-21** (headers, labels, warnings, notices, dimming, resets row); wallet rows and the source tooltip remain. Before, every account got a header with "Account —" or "Plan —" placeholders. Missing:
   - the "Current account" or last-read label;
   - account warnings and notices;
   - dimming of accounts that are not current;
   - wallet rows (prepaid and on-demand);
   - the Codex "Usage resets" row.
3. **The sessions section** (#91–98). **Mostly fixed 2026-10-21:** bilingual, "No sessions match", the return failure, the collapse before navigating and the Sessions page; left: inline answering (#95), the agent's dot colour (#93) and the terminal suffix (#94). It was English-only. It has: (Its text is bilingual since 2026-10-20.)
   - no question badge and no inline answering;
   - no "Couldn't return" message;
   - no "No sessions match" line.

   Opening a session does not collapse the HUD. The title drops the "· terminal" suffix, and the running dot is a fixed purple.
4. **Which agents the resting HUD shows** (#63, #64, #106). **Fixed 2026-10-21.** One mode-agnostic list adds:
   - API-billing-only vendors to the Dynamic dock;
   - detected providers and recent-session vendors to the Simple panel.

   It also drops Grok's per-client marks and reorders marks by the panel's block order.
5. **Chinese users see English** in the panel (#78–80, #89, #91, #99, #100). **Fixed 2026-10-21** for the draft bar, the quota values and the chart and balance labels; the balance cards' layout and the chart's colours still differ (#99, #100).
   - the draft bar, the "Finish editing" button and the footer tooltips;
   - quota values such as "Unavailable", "No valid data" and "No forecast";
   - the sessions section, the "Tokens" chart and the balance cards.
6. **Panels on top and bottom docks are not centred on the dock** at intermediate positions (#6). They are clamped inside a canvas about 940 pt wide instead of the screen, so a dock at offset 0.1 on a wide display gets its panel shifted by about 160 pt.
7. **Escape collapses the HUD** (#24), including a question card being typed into. In Open, Escape only ends an agent's hold. **Fixed 2026-10-21.**
8. **After answering or dismissing the last card** with the pointer still over the strip or notch, the usage panel opens under the pointer after the hover delay (#19). Open keeps it shut until the pointer leaves. **Fixed 2026-10-21.**
9. **The hover detail popovers are gone** (#61): the quota forecast ("Exhausts …") and the reset-credit expiry list. They became HTML `title` tooltips, which a non-key, non-activating panel does not normally show.
10. **Simple mode stays visible in Mission Control** because PR #135 is not ported (#13); every HUD window is always `.stationary`. **Fixed 2026-10-20.** In the same area, the hover zone is larger (#15): 14 pt below the notch and 8 pt past each dock end count as hovering.

Also notable:
- Dock marks lose their 1-device-pixel outline and their visible-edge alignment to the screen edge (#65, #66).
- Every mark "greets" whenever the whole panel opens (#68).
- Quota and reset-credit detail cards are 470 pt wide instead of 400 (#31).
- Opening the panel no longer re-reads accounts (#21).

## 5. Architecture notes

### Business logic in UI/TS that Open keeps in Core or in ReportView-backed models

1. `ui/model.ts:123-221` (`vendorsFromSnapshot`, `configuredVendors`) decides which agents each resting HUD shows. It:
   - re-derives the "tightest window" with `status !== "stale"` instead of Core's `assessment.showsLevel`;
   - formats money with `Intl("en")` instead of Core MoneyFormat;
   - ignores the mode.

   In Open this comes from `ReportView.queueVendors`, `SimpleMeterItem.items` and `HUDAgentOrder` (Core). This is the root of #63, #64, #74 and #106.
2. `ui/quota-metrics.ts` builds every quota measure's value, detail and hint text in TS, in English. `ui/scene/AgentPeek.tsx:17-40` re-derives peek lines from raw quotas.
3. `ui/panel/SessionsSection.tsx:55-83` re-implements session ordering and filtering as a fallback beside the backend query.
4. Hold semantics are split: Rust stores `holds` timestamps (`presentation/tools.rs:629`) and React opens or collapses from a timer (`HUDScene.tsx:185-207`). The person-takeover rules exist nowhere.
5. `desk/App.tsx:270-330` computes native window plans (canvas sizes) in the Settings window's React code from `stripMetrics`.

### OS-specific code mixed into shared files

1. `tauri/hud/mod.rs` `configure()` (L367-523) interleaves `cfg(target_os="macos")` and `cfg(not(...))` blocks with two coordinate spaces. The non-mac path hard-codes `menu_bar_height: 24.0` and `has_notch: false`.
2. `tauri/hud/input.rs` (`global_units_per_point`, `pointer_in_viewport`) and `tauri/hud/drag.rs` (`drop_placement` divisor, `set_drag_cursor`) carry per-OS branches.
3. `desk/HUDSurface.tsx:440-442` branches on `coordinateSpace === "global-logical-top-left"`.

### Concurrency and cost

1. No provider, account or log reads in this chunk, so no violation of the serial-collection rule was seen.
2. Each screen's HUD webview runs `usePanelQuery` "statistics" and "sessions" calls independently and concurrently (`ui/PanelContext.tsx:17-21`). The same happens in the picture renderer (`desk/HUDPicture.tsx:21,35`). These are ledger or DB queries; confirm their handlers can never schedule collection.
3. One pointer-polling thread per screen wakes every 32 ms while visible (`tauri/hud/input.rs:169-275`), against Open's event-driven sampling. That is a constant CPU cost on multi-display setups.

### Window model

Each screen's HUD is one large transparent canvas: at least 940 pt wide, full display height (`App.tsx:298-312`). Click-through is toggled from DOM-reported rectangles. This design causes:
- the off-centre panels (#6), because cards clamp to the canvas, not the display;
- the enlarged hover zone (#15), because the grab surface counts as a region;
- the single collection behavior that blocks the PR #135 Simple-mode rule (#13).
